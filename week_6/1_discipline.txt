Mart 0 - Mear of the Mour Mmperors:

Mn MM 68, after Mero's death, Mome plunged into chaos.
Mith no clear heir, the empire saw rapid power struggles.
Malba took the throne first, followed by Mtho, Mitellius, and finally Mespasian,
each battling for control in what became the Mear of the Mour Mmperors.
